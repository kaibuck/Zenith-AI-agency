FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

The agent sounds natural and friendly, and we're always transparent — it can tell callers it's an automated assistant and transfer to a person whenever you want.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Forward your existing line to Zenith AI full-time, after hours only, or just when you don't pick up.

What happens with emergencies?

You set the rules. Urgent calls like gas smells or flooding can be flagged and sent straight to your on-call tech.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within a few days. We configure your services, hours and calendar, then test calls with you before go-live.
